How to play

    

      
1
Look
Eight things are already sorted into In and Out. A secret rule decides which is which.

      
2
Test
Type anything you like and see whether it's in or out. The fewer tests you need, the more stars you get.

      
3
Prove
Think you've got it? Sort six new things. Get all six right and you're done.

    

    
Tip. The first rule that comes to mind is usually wrong. Test things you think are out.

    
Numbers, words and shapes take turns through the week.
